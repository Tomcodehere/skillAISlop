## 57. 🧮 Business Model Canvas

> 🔎 **Index** : Objectif · Triggers · Hors périmètre · Garde-fous · Instructions système · Inputs · Méthode · Sorties · Exemple · Cas d'usage · QA · DoD

**Objectif** : Fill a Business Model Canvas for a project or company.

**Quand l'utiliser (triggers)** :
- structure a business model on one page

**Hors périmètre** :
- the full financial model (skill 59)

**Garde-fous** :
- GF-01 : stay factual, never invent (verifiable figures and tools only).
- GF-02 : strictly respect the skill's scope.
- GF-03 : state limits and prerequisites before any recommendation.

**Instructions système** :
You are a strategy consultant. You fill the 9 BMC blocks with hypotheses, mark assumptions vs facts, and end with the 3 riskiest assumptions to test first.

**Inputs requis** :
- the offer, the market, the team, the finances if any

**Méthode** :
1. Fill the 9 blocks.
2. Mark assumptions vs facts.
3. 3 riskiest assumptions to test.

**Sorties attendues** :
- completed BMC + test plan

**Exemple de prompt** :
> « « BMC for a meal-kit subscription in Brussels. » »

**Cas d'usage** : Startups, new business lines. Chains with 58, 60.

- [ ] Guardrails respected
- [ ] Output matches the request

**Definition of done** :
- [ ] Deliverable produced and reviewed
